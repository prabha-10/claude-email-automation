# Team lessons

Generic lessons learned from real builds and Litmus runs. The build-email skill reads this file before it writes MJML. The test-email skill reads it before it plans a fix. The find_lessons tool searches it.

If a fix helps any future email, add a lesson. Do not limit it to one client. Write one bullet each: the problem, the fix, and which client showed it.

- (example) Gmail App dark mode: a dark logo on a transparent PNG disappears. Fix: add a light outline to the dark parts, or give the logo a solid background. Seen in Gmail App Dark.

## Fix log

One line per fix that was tried. Failed fixes stay here, so nobody tries them again. The kind is worked, failed, inconclusive or idea (known, not tried yet). The lines below come from the first two real builds and from outside research on 2026-11-24. "Not tested by us" means a source says the fix works and nobody here has seen it work.

- [failed] Outlook 2021, Outlook 365 Dark (Windows desktop) | Light text turns dark grey. A yellow headline turns olive. A dark footer turns light grey. | Fix: [data-ogsc] and [data-ogsb] colour overrides. | Cause: Those attributes work only in Outlook.com and the Outlook mobile apps. Outlook on Windows desktop inverts colours after the email CSS runs. | Evidence: Litmus dark-mode support chart. No change in the OL365 Dark and Outlook 2021 Dark renders. | black-friday v2, 2026-11-24
- [idea] Outlook 2021, Outlook 365 Dark (Windows desktop) | Light text or dark areas get inverted | Fix: Put a VML fill (v:rect with v:fill type solid) behind the dark area. Or use the text-fill gradient (mso-style-textfill-type:gradient) with a fallback colour that inverts to the wanted colour. Or make key text an image. | Cause: Classic Outlook recolours text and plain backgrounds. It does not recolour VML fills or images. Microsoft names no supported way to opt out. | Evidence: Tuts+ article "How to fix Outlook dark mode problems" and the Courier dark-mode guide. Not tested by us. Results differ between Litmus and a real PC. | research 2026-11-24
- [failed] Outlook 2021 Dark, Outlook 365 Dark, Gmail App Dark iOS | A dark footer turns light grey | Fix: A VML dark tile (mj-section background-url) and the gradient text trick. | Cause: Outlook does not recolour VML fills, so the footer stayed dark there. MJML also writes the tile as a CSS background. Gmail App iOS dark inverts the text but not that image, so the text turned dark on the dark tile. The trick sets one colour for light and dark mode. | Evidence: Litmus footer test with three variants. Outlook dark kept the dark footer. Gmail App iOS dark showed dark text on near-black. A footer link was nearly invisible in Outlook dark. In Outlook 2021 light, the text moved about 24px left and the yellow headline turned white. | footer test v2, 2026-11-25
- [failed] Outlook 2021 Dark, Outlook 365 Dark (Windows desktop) | A dark footer turns light grey in Outlook dark mode | Fix: A VML dark tile plus mso-color-alt:auto on the text. | Cause: Outlook picked dark text on the dark fill. | Evidence: Litmus footer test: dark, hard-to-read text in Outlook dark. | footer test v2, 2026-11-25
- [idea] Outlook 2021 Dark, Outlook 365 Dark (Windows desktop) | A dark footer turns light grey in Outlook dark mode | Fix: Write the VML tile for Outlook only, inside an mso conditional comment in mj-raw, with no background-url. Give each design text colour its own gradient class. | Cause: Gmail then never sees the tile, and each text keeps its own colour. | Evidence: Not tested by us. Test it as a variant next to the plain footer, in Outlook light and dark. | after the footer test, 2026-11-25
- [worked] Gmail (Chrome), iPhone 17 | The last word of a headline sits alone on its own line | Fix: Wrap the last two words in a span with class nowrap (the .nowrap class sets white-space:nowrap). | Cause: Gmail's fallback font is wider than the web font, so the line wraps before the last word. | Evidence: Litmus round 2 of three emails. | 2026-11-25
- [failed] Gmail App Dark Android | Dark green text on a mid-tone yellow band turns pale mint and is hard to read | Fix: Set the same yellow as a cell background colour (v2). Set it also as a tiny background image (v3). | Cause: Gmail Android lightens very dark text and leaves mid-tone backgrounds alone. It changes text and background separately, so a protected background does not protect the text. | Evidence: black-friday v2 and v3 renders. The v3 image also made the band look two-tone. Better.email and Dyspatch dark-mode articles. | black-friday v3, 2026-11-24
- [idea] Gmail App Dark Android and iOS | Gmail recolours an email that has no dark version | Fix: Add the tag meta name="color-scheme" content="light only". Add a style block with :root{color-scheme:light dark}, so Apple Mail keeps its dark styles. | Cause: Dyspatch and emailmarkup.org report that Google fixed the Gmail apps in late 2026 to honour light-only, for Google accounts only. Stensul reports that the apps still recolour. Litmus Gmail previews can still show forced dark while real sends do not. | Evidence: Not tested by us. Send to a real phone before you trust a Litmus result. | research 2026-11-24
- [idea] Gmail App Dark Android | Dark text on a coloured band that Gmail recolours | Fix: Put the band text in an image with the band colour baked in, and keep the alt text. Or use a text colour that is not very dark on a mid-tone band. | Cause: Same as above. The image is the fallback for accounts that ignore light-only. | Evidence: Better.email and Dyspatch articles. Not tested by us. | research 2026-11-24
- [failed] Outlook 2021 | White specks on the near-black areas of a JPEG photo | Fix: Higher JPEG quality. Standard 4:2:0 colour sampling. Lifting pure black to about luma 5. A lossless PNG of the photo. | Cause: Not found. Image format, quality and sampling are ruled out. | Evidence: email-7 rounds 2 to 4. The specks stayed at 100 and 120 DPI. | email-7 v4, 2026-11-24
- [inconclusive] Outlook 2021 | White specks on the near-black areas of a JPEG photo | Fix: A copy of the photo saved at exactly the display width, so Word does not resize it. | Cause: Word resizing is not the main cause. | Evidence: email-7 v5 showed fewer specks at 100% DPI only. black-friday v3 showed no change at 100 DPI, 120 DPI and dark. | black-friday v3, 2026-11-24
- [idea] Outlook 2021 | White specks on the near-black areas of a JPEG photo | Fix: Raise the darkest pixels of the photo to about luma 18 with the lift_blacks tool, then test the copy as a variant. Also open the same image in a real Outlook 2021, to rule out the Litmus screenshot. | Cause: This is a lead only. The specks sit on near-black pixels. 15% of the black-friday lower photo is at luma 6 or lower. The upper photo has a darkest luma of 17 and shows none. | Evidence: Measured on black-friday images/hero-bottom.jpg and hero-top.jpg. Not confirmed. | black-friday, 2026-11-24

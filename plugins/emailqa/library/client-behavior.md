# Email client dark-mode behaviour

This file says what each email client does to an email in dark mode, and which fixes developers report. The build-email skill reads it before it styles colours, logos and dark areas. The `find_lessons` tool searches it line by line.

Checked 2026-11-25. Email clients change their dark-mode code without notice. Look at the source date in a bullet before you trust it.

How to read a bullet. Each bullet names the client app and the symptom, then the cause, the fix and the source. "Tested by us" means that a real Litmus render in `lessons.md` shows it. "Not tested by us" means that only the source says it. When sources disagree, the bullet names both sides. "No documented cause" means that we found no source for a cause.

Dark-mode types in this file:

| Type | What the client does |
| --- | --- |
| No change | The client shows the email colours as designed. |
| Partial invert | The client darkens light backgrounds and lightens dark text. It leaves dark areas alone. |
| Full invert | The client also turns dark backgrounds light and light text dark. |
| Own dark styles | The client uses the dark styles in the email (`@media (prefers-color-scheme: dark)`). |

Summary. The bullets below give the source for each cell.

| Client | Dark-mode type | `prefers-color-scheme` | `[data-ogsc]`, `[data-ogsb]` | `color-scheme` meta tag |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Apple Mail macOS, iOS | No change until the email opts in, then own dark styles | Yes | No | Yes, it is the opt-in (caniemail iOS data is from iOS 12.4) |
| Gmail web | No change | No | No | No |
| Gmail App Android | Partial invert | No | No | `light only` only, Google accounts, sources disagree |
| Gmail App iOS | Full invert | No | No | `light only` only, Google accounts, sources disagree |
| Classic Outlook Windows 2021, 365 | Full invert | No | No (tested by us) | No |
| New Outlook for Windows | Not documented, sources group it with Outlook.com | Not documented | Not documented | Not documented |
| Outlook.com | Partial invert | Sources disagree | Yes | No |
| Outlook iOS, Android | Partial invert | Sources disagree | Yes | No |
| Outlook macOS | Partial invert | Yes or partial | Yes | No |
| Yahoo Mail, AOL | No change on web, apps disputed | No | No | No |
| Samsung Email | Sources disagree | Yes | No | No |

## Apple Mail (macOS, iOS)

- Apple Mail macOS and iOS, dark mode: the email keeps its light colours until the email opts in. Cause: without the `color-scheme` meta tags, Apple Mail does not change modes. Fix: for a dark version, add the meta tags and `@media (prefers-color-scheme: dark)` styles. Source: https://www.litmus.com/blog/the-ultimate-guide-to-dark-mode-for-email-marketers (Litmus, 2025-02-27). Not tested by us.
- Apple Mail macOS and iOS, dark mode: dark text disappears on areas without a background colour. Cause: Apple Mail shows its own dark window behind areas with no background. Fix: set a background colour on `mj-body` and on every section. Source: https://www.courier.com/blog/dark-mode-email-design (Courier, updated 2026-11-06). Not tested by us.
- Apple Mail macOS and iOS, dark mode: Apple Mail honours `@media (prefers-color-scheme: dark)`. caniemail lists support in Mail 16 on macOS and Mail 16.1 on iOS. The `color-scheme` CSS property works only on the `:root` or `html` element. Source: https://www.caniemail.com/features/css-at-media-prefers-color-scheme/ and https://www.caniemail.com/features/css-color-scheme/ (caniemail, tested 2023). Not tested by us.
- Apple Mail macOS and iOS, dark mode: an email with the `color-scheme` meta tag and no dark styles gets a partial invert. Pure white `#ffffff` backgrounds turn dark. Fix: if the email has no dark styles of its own, leave out the meta tags. Source: https://www.litmus.com/blog/the-ultimate-guide-to-dark-mode-for-email-marketers (Litmus, 2025-02-27). Not tested by us.
- Apple Mail macOS and iOS, dark mode: pure white `#ffffff` and pure black `#000000` text or backgrounds flip, and near values stay. Fix: for a white or black that must not flip, use `#fffffe` or `#fdfdfd` for white and `#010101` for black. Source: https://www.litmus.com/blog/coding-emails-for-dark-mode (Litmus, 2024-10-23), https://www.emailonacid.com/blog/article/email-development/dark-mode-for-email/ (Email on Acid, 2025-10-02), https://noti.st/hteumeuleu/QhIGkB/slides (Remi Parmentier, 2022-09-06). Not tested by us.
- Apple Mail macOS and iOS, dark mode: a dark logo on a transparent PNG disappears on the dark background. Cause: the dark background shows through the transparent pixels. Fix: give the logo a thin light outline or glow, or swap a light and a dark logo in the `prefers-color-scheme` query. Source: https://www.litmus.com/blog/the-ultimate-guide-to-dark-mode-for-email-marketers (Litmus, 2025-02-27), https://stensul.com/stream/5-must-dos-when-designing-emails-for-dark-mode/ (Stensul, 2026-11-20). Not tested by us.
- Apple Mail macOS and iOS, dark mode: the meta tag `<meta name="color-scheme" content="light only">` for Gmail also stops the Apple Mail dark styles. Fix: add `:root { color-scheme: light dark; }` in a style block to keep the Apple Mail dark styles. Source: https://emailmarkup.org/en/blog/2026/gmail-app-dark-mode-update/ (Email Markup Consortium, 2026-09-21), https://www.dyspatch.io/blog/gmail-now-respects-your-color-scheme-how-to-stop-forced-dark-mode-in-the-gmail-app/ (Dyspatch, 2026-10-08). Not tested by us.

## Gmail web

- Gmail web (desktop browser), dark mode: Gmail leaves the email body colours alone. It ignores `@media (prefers-color-scheme)`, the `color-scheme` property and the `color-scheme` meta tag. Fix: none needed. Source: https://www.courier.com/blog/dark-mode-email-design (Courier, updated 2026-11-06), https://www.caniemail.com/features/html-meta-color-scheme/ (caniemail, tested 2026-09-16), https://stensul.com/stream/5-must-dos-when-designing-emails-for-dark-mode/ (Stensul, 2026-11-20). Not tested by us.

## Gmail App Android

- Gmail App Android, dark mode: Gmail applies a partial invert. It darkens light backgrounds and lightens dark text, and it leaves dark areas such as buttons alone. Cause: emailmarkup.org says that the Android app uses Chromium algorithmic darkening. Source: https://www.litmus.com/blog/the-ultimate-guide-to-dark-mode-for-email-marketers (Litmus, 2025-02-27), https://emailmarkup.org/en/blog/2026/gmail-app-dark-mode-update/ (Email Markup Consortium, 2026-09-21). Not tested by us.
- Gmail App Android, dark mode: very dark text on a mid-tone coloured band turns pale and hard to read. Dark green text on a mid-tone yellow band turned pale mint. Cause: Gmail lightens the dark text and does not darken the mid-tone background. American Express says that Gmail does not darken a background darker than about 20% grey. Fix: do not put very dark text on a band darker than about 20% grey. Or put the band text in an image with the band colour, and keep the copy in the alt text. Source: lessons.md Fix log (black-friday v2 and v3), https://designinternet-qaeusw1green.americanexpress.com/community/marketing-email-design-system/foundations/dark-mode (American Express email design system, read through a search result only). Tested by us (the symptom). The fixes are not tested by us.
- Gmail App Android, dark mode: the band colour as a cell background colour does not stop Gmail from lightening the dark band text. The band colour as a small tiled background image fails too, and it made the band look two-tone. Cause: lessons.md (from Better.email and Dyspatch articles) says that Gmail changes text and background separately. Fix: do not try these again. Source: lessons.md Fix log (black-friday v2 and v3). Tested by us (failed).
- Gmail App Android and iOS, dark mode, background images: Gmail still inverts a tiled background image smaller than 60 by 60 px. Fix: make a colour-holding background tile at least 60 by 60 px. No source links this to the two-tone band in black-friday v3. Source: https://parcel.io/guides/color-scheme-in-email (Parcel, no date). Not tested by us.
- Gmail App Android and iOS, dark mode: sources disagree on the tag `<meta name="color-scheme" content="light only">` as a stop for forced dark mode in Google accounts. For: caniemail (tested 2026-09-16) lists partial support for the `light only` value only. Email Markup Consortium says Google marked the issue fixed on 2026-08-31 for Android and iOS. Dyspatch says real sends showed the light design. Against: Stensul says that the Gmail apps recolour the email even with a light-only scheme. Fix: check it with a real send to a phone with a Google account, not with Litmus. Source: https://www.caniemail.com/features/html-meta-color-scheme/, https://emailmarkup.org/en/blog/2026/gmail-app-dark-mode-update/ (2026-09-21), https://www.dyspatch.io/blog/gmail-now-respects-your-color-scheme-how-to-stop-forced-dark-mode-in-the-gmail-app/ (2026-10-08), https://stensul.com/stream/5-must-dos-when-designing-emails-for-dark-mode/ (2026-11-20). Not tested by us.
- Gmail App Android and iOS with a non-Google account (for example Yahoo or Outlook.com in the Gmail app), dark mode: Gmail removes `<style>` blocks and ignores `mix-blend-mode`. The `light only` meta tag does not help these accounts. Source: https://www.hteumeuleu.com/2021/fixing-gmail-dark-mode-css-blend-modes/ (Remi Parmentier, 2021-04-09), https://www.caniemail.com/features/css-mix-blend-mode/ (caniemail), https://emailmarkup.org/en/blog/2026/gmail-app-dark-mode-update/ (2026-09-21). Not tested by us.
- Gmail App Android and iOS, dark mode: Gmail ignores `@media (prefers-color-scheme: dark)` and `[data-ogsc]`, so the email cannot give Gmail its own dark styles. Source: https://www.caniemail.com/features/css-at-media-prefers-color-scheme/ (caniemail, Gmail tested 2022-12), https://www.litmus.com/blog/the-ultimate-guide-to-dark-mode-for-email-marketers (Litmus, 2025-02-27). Not tested by us.
- Gmail App Android and iOS, dark mode: Gmail changes `background-color` but not `background-image`. A one-colour `linear-gradient` background keeps its colour, but the text colour can still change. Source: https://noti.st/hteumeuleu/QhIGkB/slides (Remi Parmentier, 2022-09-06), https://www.hteumeuleu.com/2021/fixing-gmail-dark-mode-css-blend-modes/ (2021-04-09). Not tested by us.
- Gmail App Android and iOS, dark mode: white text on a coloured background turns dark. Fix: the blend-mode hack. Wrap the text in two `div` elements with `u + .body .gmail-blend-screen` and `u + .body .gmail-blend-difference` styles over a `linear-gradient` background. It keeps white text white only, and only for Google accounts. Source: https://www.hteumeuleu.com/2021/fixing-gmail-dark-mode-css-blend-modes/ (Remi Parmentier, 2021-04-09), https://www.courier.com/blog/dark-mode-email-design (Courier, updated 2026-11-06). Not tested by us.
- Gmail App Android and iOS, dark mode, images and transparent PNGs: a dark logo on a transparent PNG disappears on the darkened background. Sources disagree on photos and artwork. Stensul says that clients do not recolour the pixels in images. Courier says that Gmail can put a colour filter on PNG artwork. Fix: a thin light outline or glow on the logo, or an opaque plate behind it. Source: https://stensul.com/stream/5-must-dos-when-designing-emails-for-dark-mode/ (Stensul, 2026-11-20), https://www.courier.com/blog/dark-mode-email-design (Courier, updated 2026-11-06). Not tested by us.
- Gmail App Android and iOS, dark mode, image swap: Gmail replaces a `<picture>` element with `<u></u>`, so the image disappears. Fix: swap light and dark images with wrapper `div` elements, not `<picture>`. Source: https://www.courier.com/blog/dark-mode-email-design (Courier, updated 2026-11-06). Not tested by us.

## Gmail App iOS

- Gmail App iOS, dark mode: Gmail applies a full invert. It also turns dark backgrounds light and light text dark, even in an email that is already dark. Cause: emailmarkup.org says that the iOS app uses luminance inversion. Source: https://www.litmus.com/blog/the-ultimate-guide-to-dark-mode-for-email-marketers (Litmus, 2025-02-27), https://www.hteumeuleu.com/2021/fixing-gmail-dark-mode-css-blend-modes/ (2021-04-09), https://emailmarkup.org/en/blog/2026/gmail-app-dark-mode-update/ (2026-09-21). Not tested by us.
- Gmail App iOS, dark mode: Gmail rewrites dark backgrounds and dark button fills too. Fix: choose fills dark enough to stay readable after the change, or use the blend-mode hack for white text. Source: https://www.courier.com/blog/dark-mode-email-design (Courier, updated 2026-11-06). Not tested by us.
- Gmail App iOS, dark mode: on an email designed dark, the `light only` meta tag stops the inversion of the dark design, for Google accounts. Source: https://emailmarkup.org/en/blog/2026/gmail-app-dark-mode-update/ (Email Markup Consortium, 2026-09-21). Not tested by us.

## Classic Outlook on Windows (2016, 2019, 2021, 365)

- Outlook 2021 and Outlook 365 (Office 365) on Windows, dark mode: Outlook applies a full invert to text and background colours. Light text turned dark grey, a yellow headline turned olive, and a dark footer turned light grey. Cause: with the Black Office theme, classic Outlook recolours text and plain backgrounds. Source: lessons.md Fix log (black-friday v2), https://www.litmus.com/blog/the-ultimate-guide-to-dark-mode-for-email-marketers (Litmus, 2025-02-27), https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/office/dark-mode-in-outlook-3e2446e0-9a7b-4189-9af9-57fb94d02ae3 (Microsoft). Tested by us.
- Outlook 2016 and Outlook 2019 on Windows, dark mode: the sources that we found describe dark message bodies for Outlook 2021 and Outlook 365 only. No source describes Outlook 2016 or 2019 separately. caniemail lists no `prefers-color-scheme` support up to Outlook 2019. Source: https://www.caniemail.com/features/css-at-media-prefers-color-scheme/ (caniemail), https://www.litmus.com/blog/coding-emails-for-dark-mode (Litmus, 2024-10-23). Not tested by us.
- Outlook 2021 and Outlook 365 on Windows, dark mode: `[data-ogsc]` and `[data-ogsb]` colour overrides change nothing. Cause: Outlook.com, Outlook mobile and Outlook macOS add these attributes, and Outlook on Windows does not. Source: lessons.md Fix log (black-friday v2), https://www.litmus.com/blog/the-ultimate-guide-to-dark-mode-for-email-marketers (Litmus chart, 2025-02), https://www.caniemail.com/features/css-at-media-prefers-color-scheme/ (caniemail note 3). Tested by us (failed).
- Outlook 2016, 2019, 2021 and 365 on Windows, dark mode: Outlook ignores `@media (prefers-color-scheme: dark)`, the `color-scheme` meta tag and the `color-scheme` property. A Microsoft employee reported on Microsoft Q&A that the media query styles do not show in Outlook for Microsoft 365. Source: https://www.caniemail.com/features/css-at-media-prefers-color-scheme/, https://www.caniemail.com/features/html-meta-color-scheme/ (caniemail), https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/answers/questions/1432508/does-outlook-for-microsoft-365-support-emails-with (Microsoft Q&A, 2023-11-20). Not tested by us.
- Outlook 2021 and Outlook 365 on Windows, dark mode: Courier says that Microsoft names no supported way to stop the recolouring. Courier lists `!important`, mso conditional comments, `prefers-color-scheme` and `color-scheme: light only` as failed. Litmus says that no coding workaround exists. Tuts+ describes partial hacks in the bullets below. Source: https://www.courier.com/blog/dark-mode-email-design (Courier, updated 2026-11-06), https://www.litmus.com/blog/coding-emails-for-dark-mode (Litmus, 2024-10-23). Not tested by us.
- Outlook 2021 and Outlook 365 on Windows, dark mode, VML and background images: Outlook does not recolour images or VML fills. It still recolours text inside a VML shape. Fix: keep a dark area dark with a VML fill or a background image. In MJML, `mj-section` with `background-url` writes a `v:rect` with a `v:fill`. Courier says that a background image can carry a colour, but the result differs between Outlook builds. Source: https://webdesign.tutsplus.com/how-to-fix-outlook-dark-mode-problems--cms-37718t (Nicole Merlin, Tuts+, 2023-09-01), https://www.courier.com/blog/dark-mode-email-design (Courier, updated 2026-11-06). Tested by us (Litmus footer test, 2026-11-25): with `background-url`, the dark tile kept the footer dark in Outlook 2021 and 365 dark. But MJML also writes the tile as a CSS background, and Gmail App iOS dark then showed dark text on the dark tile.
- Outlook 2021 and Outlook 365 on Windows, dark mode: white text turns dark. Fix (gradient text trick): put a class in a `<!--[if gte mso 16]>` style block with `mso-style-textfill-type:gradient`, a white gradient stop list and `color:#000000 !important`. Outlook drops the gradient in dark mode and inverts the black fallback to white. The class must be the only class on the element. Tuts+ says that the trick is not reliable inside VML in Litmus. Source: https://webdesign.tutsplus.com/how-to-fix-outlook-dark-mode-problems--cms-37718t (Nicole Merlin, Tuts+, 2023-09-01). Tested by us with a VML dark tile (Litmus, 2026-11-25): the text stayed white in Outlook 2021 and 365 dark. In Outlook 2021 light, the yellow headline also turned white, because the class sets one colour for light and dark. The text also moved about 24px left.
- Outlook 2021 and Outlook 365 on Windows, dark mode: white text on a dark VML fill turns dark. Fix (experimental `color:auto` trick): give the text `mso-color-alt:auto`. Tuts+ gives the rule. With a body background of `#555555` or lighter and a VML fill of `#333333` or darker, Outlook shows white text. Only one auto colour, white or black, works in one email. Source: https://webdesign.tutsplus.com/how-to-fix-outlook-dark-mode-problems--cms-37718t (Nicole Merlin, Tuts+, 2023-09-01). Tested by us with a VML dark tile (Litmus, 2026-11-25): failed. The text stayed dark in Outlook dark.
- Outlook 2021 and Outlook 365 on Windows, dark mode: text in an image keeps its colours, so the image shows as a bright rectangle on the inverted background. Fix: keep text live where you can. Source: https://stensul.com/stream/5-must-dos-when-designing-emails-for-dark-mode/ (Stensul, 2026-11-20). Not tested by us.
- Outlook 2019, 2021 and 365 on Windows, dark mode, transparent PNGs: a dark logo or icon on a transparent PNG disappears on the inverted dark background. Fix: add a translucent or light outline to the dark parts. Source: https://www.litmus.com/blog/the-ultimate-guide-to-dark-mode-for-email-marketers (Litmus, 2025-02-27), https://stensul.com/stream/5-must-dos-when-designing-emails-for-dark-mode/ (Stensul, 2026-11-20). Not tested by us.
- Outlook 2021 on Windows, light and dark mode: white specks show on the near-black areas of a JPEG photo. Cause: No documented cause. We ruled out JPEG quality, colour sampling, a lossless PNG copy and Word resizing. A lead (not proven): the specks sit on pixels at luma 6 or lower. A Strategy knowledge base title also reports white dots on a dark background in Outlook. That page returned HTTP 403 to us. Source: lessons.md Fix log (email-7 v2 to v5, black-friday v3), https://community.strategy.com/article/KB226307-Using-an-email-subscription-for-the-document-with-a (Strategy, not read). Tested by us (the symptom).
- Outlook for Microsoft 365 on Windows (classic), dark mode: the reader controls dark message bodies. The sun and moon button switches one message between white and black. The option "Never change the message background color" keeps every message white. Source: https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/office/dark-mode-in-outlook-3e2446e0-9a7b-4189-9af9-57fb94d02ae3 (Microsoft). Not tested by us.

## New Outlook for Windows

- New Outlook for Windows, dark mode: no source documents its dark-mode rules for email bodies. Courier says that it uses the Outlook.com rendering in WebView2, and calls this an inference. Stensul groups it with Outlook.com as a partial invert with `[data-ogsc]` support. Fix: build for Outlook.com and test the new Outlook in Litmus. Source: https://www.courier.com/blog/dark-mode-email-design (Courier, updated 2026-11-06), https://stensul.com/stream/5-must-dos-when-designing-emails-for-dark-mode/ (Stensul, 2026-11-20), https://noti.st/hteumeuleu/QhIGkB/slides (Remi Parmentier, 2022-09-06). Not tested by us.
- New Outlook for Windows, dark mode: the reader can press "Turn on the lights" in the reading pane, or choose View, Light Reading Pane, to see the light design. Source: https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/office/dark-mode-in-outlook-3e2446e0-9a7b-4189-9af9-57fb94d02ae3 (Microsoft). Not tested by us.

## Outlook.com

- Outlook.com, dark mode: Outlook.com applies a partial invert. It changes a text or background colour that has less than 4.5:1 contrast with its dark background, and leaves other colours. Source: https://www.hteumeuleu.com/2021/emails-react-outlook-com-dark-mode/ (Remi Parmentier, 2021-02-26), https://www.courier.com/blog/dark-mode-email-design (Courier, updated 2026-11-06). Not tested by us.
- Outlook.com, dark mode, `[data-ogsc]` and `[data-ogsb]`: Outlook.com keeps the original colours in `data-ogsc`, `data-ogac`, `data-ogsb` and `data-ogab` attributes. Fix: write `[data-ogsc] .class` rules for text and `[data-ogsb] .class` rules for backgrounds. Repeat the attribute on every selector in a list. Outlook.com supports only `[attr]` and `E[attr]`, so `.class[data-ogsb]` fails. Source: https://www.hteumeuleu.com/2021/emails-react-outlook-com-dark-mode/ (2021-02-26), https://www.litmus.com/blog/coding-emails-for-dark-mode (Litmus, 2024-10-23), https://www.courier.com/blog/dark-mode-email-design (Courier, updated 2026-11-06). Not tested by us.
- Outlook.com, dark mode: Outlook.com writes its new colours inline with `!important`, so a rule on the same element cannot override them. Fix: put the colour on a nested element. Source: https://parcel.io/guides/color-scheme-in-email (Parcel, no date). Not tested by us.
- Outlook.com, dark mode, `prefers-color-scheme`: sources disagree. caniemail (2022-12) and Courier say that Outlook.com supports `@media (prefers-color-scheme: dark)`. The Litmus chart (2025-02) says that it does not. Source: https://www.caniemail.com/features/css-at-media-prefers-color-scheme/, https://www.courier.com/blog/dark-mode-email-design, https://www.litmus.com/blog/the-ultimate-guide-to-dark-mode-for-email-marketers. Not tested by us.
- Outlook.com, dark mode: the light and dark toggle of the reader can show the dark version, even with light mode selected. Parmentier says that the toggle does not change styles from the media query. Source: https://www.litmus.com/blog/the-ultimate-guide-to-dark-mode-for-email-marketers (Litmus, 2025-02-27), https://noti.st/hteumeuleu/QhIGkB/slides (2022-09-06). Not tested by us.
- Outlook.com, dark mode, images and transparent PNGs: Outlook.com can cut transparent images into pieces, and transparent social icons become hard to read. For a light and dark image swap in Outlook.com, put each image inside a link. Source: https://noti.st/hteumeuleu/QhIGkB/slides (Remi Parmentier, 2022-09-06), https://www.litmus.com/blog/coding-emails-for-dark-mode (Litmus, 2024-10-23). Not tested by us.

## Outlook iOS and Android

- Outlook iOS and Outlook Android, dark mode: the apps apply a partial invert. They darken light backgrounds and lighten dark text. They can also darken light text on dark backgrounds. Customer.io says that the iOS app is a little darker than the Android app. Source: https://www.litmus.com/blog/the-ultimate-guide-to-dark-mode-for-email-marketers (Litmus, 2025-02-27), https://customer.io/learn/message-composing/email-forced-dark-mode (Customer.io, no date). Not tested by us.
- Outlook iOS and Outlook Android, dark mode, images: Microsoft says that the apps do not convert images to dark mode. A dark logo on a transparent PNG disappears. Fix: a thin light outline on the logo. Source: https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/office/i-m-seeing-email-content-like-images-and-highlighting-that-doesn-t-look-great-in-dark-mode-bda3c57e-68b0-480b-8bf5-17f61ec95845 (Microsoft). Not tested by us.
- Outlook iOS and Outlook Android, dark mode: `[data-ogsc]` and `[data-ogsb]` rules work, as in Outlook.com. Sources disagree on `@media (prefers-color-scheme: dark)`. caniemail lists support (iOS 2022-12, Android 2023-03). The Litmus chart (2025-02) says no. Source: https://www.caniemail.com/features/css-at-media-prefers-color-scheme/, https://www.litmus.com/blog/coding-emails-for-dark-mode, https://www.litmus.com/blog/the-ultimate-guide-to-dark-mode-for-email-marketers. Not tested by us.

## Outlook macOS

- Outlook macOS (Outlook for Mac, Office 365 macOS), dark mode: Outlook for Mac applies a partial invert and darkens light backgrounds. caniemail lists `@media (prefers-color-scheme: dark)` support in version 16.80, with the `data-ogsc` attributes. Litmus lists the media query as partial. The `color-scheme` meta tag and property have no effect. Source: https://www.caniemail.com/features/css-at-media-prefers-color-scheme/, https://www.caniemail.com/features/html-meta-color-scheme/ (caniemail), https://www.litmus.com/blog/the-ultimate-guide-to-dark-mode-for-email-marketers (Litmus, 2025-02-27). Not tested by us.

## Yahoo Mail and AOL

- Yahoo Mail web and AOL web, dark mode: the email colours do not change. Yahoo and AOL rewrite `@media (prefers-color-scheme)` into a query that never applies, so dark styles do nothing. Source: https://www.caniemail.com/features/css-at-media-prefers-color-scheme/ (caniemail notes 1 and 6), https://www.emailonacid.com/blog/article/email-development/dark-mode-for-email/ (Email on Acid, 2025-10-02), https://stensul.com/stream/5-must-dos-when-designing-emails-for-dark-mode/ (Stensul, 2026-11-20). Not tested by us.
- Yahoo Mail app iOS and Android, dark mode: sources disagree. Knak says that the Yahoo apps darken bright colours and backgrounds and lighten text. Courier, Email on Acid and Stensul list Yahoo as no change. caniemail lists no media query support in the apps. Not tested by us.

## Samsung Email

- Samsung Email (Android), dark mode: Samsung Email honours `@media (prefers-color-scheme: dark)` from version 6.1 and the `color-scheme` CSS property. It ignores the `color-scheme` meta tag. Source: https://www.caniemail.com/features/css-at-media-prefers-color-scheme/, https://www.caniemail.com/features/css-color-scheme/, https://www.caniemail.com/features/html-meta-color-scheme/ (caniemail). Not tested by us.
- Samsung Email (Android), dark mode: sources disagree on forced colour changes. Customer.io says that it changes colours like Gmail Android, and that `color-scheme` works except with Microsoft accounts. Courier says that its inversion is not documented. Source: https://customer.io/learn/message-composing/email-forced-dark-mode (Customer.io, no date), https://www.courier.com/blog/dark-mode-email-design (Courier, updated 2026-11-06). Not tested by us.

## Litmus previews and real devices

- Litmus Gmail App Dark previews (Android and iOS), `light only` meta tag: Dyspatch saw forced dark mode in the Litmus Gmail previews. Real sends to the Gmail app showed the light design. Fix: check a `light only` result with a real send to a phone with a Google account. Source: https://www.dyspatch.io/blog/gmail-now-respects-your-color-scheme-how-to-stop-forced-dark-mode-in-the-gmail-app/ (Dyspatch, 2026-10-08). Not tested by us.
- Litmus Gmail App Dark previews (Android and iOS), account type: the Litmus dark-mode post does not say how Litmus makes the previews or which account type the Gmail inboxes use. A Gmail app with a non-Google account removes `<style>` and ignores `light only`, so the account type changes the result. Source: https://www.litmus.com/blog/new-in-litmus-extended-dark-mode-testing (Litmus, 2020-09-11), https://www.hteumeuleu.com/2021/fixing-gmail-dark-mode-css-blend-modes/ (2021-04-09). Not tested by us.
- Litmus OL365 Dark and Outlook 2021 Dark previews (Outlook Windows): Tuts+ got different results in Litmus, Email on Acid, Testi and a real Windows 10 PC. In Litmus Outlook, the gradient text trick inside VML showed the fallback colour unchanged. Fix: check an Outlook Windows dark fix on a real PC before you record it as worked. Source: https://webdesign.tutsplus.com/how-to-fix-outlook-dark-mode-problems--cms-37718t (Nicole Merlin, Tuts+, 2023-09-01). Not tested by us.
- Litmus Outlook 2021 previews, white specks on near-black JPEG photo areas: we did not rule out the Litmus screenshot as the cause. Fix: open the same image in a real Outlook 2021 on a PC. Source: lessons.md Fix log (black-friday, idea). Tested by us (the symptom shows in Litmus).
- Local `preview_email` dark frames (Apple Mail, Gmail App Android, classic Outlook Windows): the frames approximate the colour change of each client. They are not renders. Fix: use them to find problems early, and use Litmus or a real device to prove a fix. Source: the `preview_email` tool description in `src/email-build.mjs`. Not tested by us.
